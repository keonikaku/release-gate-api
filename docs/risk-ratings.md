# Risk ratings

**Assigned by the author, not inferred.** A risk rating answers one question:
what would it cost if this rule were wrong in production. That is a judgement
about the business, not a property of the code, so nothing in this repository
derives it. The rows below stay `pending` until they are filled in, and the
risk based coverage view on the published site does not appear until they are.

The rating drives nothing automatically. It is there so that coverage can be
read against consequence: three cases on a low risk rule and one case on a high
risk rule is a suite that is busy in the wrong place, and that is only visible
when the two columns sit side by side.

Ratings: `high`, `medium`, `low`. Anything else counts as unrated.
